## 1. YUZU (ゆず)

Korea history: I found no record of a previous Korea show. The 2025 Asia tour (GET BACK) played Hong Kong, Shanghai and Taipei only (https://lp.p.pia.jp/article/news/435592/index.html). WONDERLIVET 2026 looks like their first Korea appearance, so there are effectively no Korean fan write-ups.

**대표곡 후보**
1. **夏色**: their signature live song, played at almost every show. On setlist.fm it is among their most-played songs (stats: https://www.setlist.fm/stats/yuzu-23d098b3.html). There is an official 2025 live clip from GET BACK トビラ: https://www.youtube.com/shorts/iKER2A9aR7g
2. **栄光の架橋**: the best-known song for general audiences (2004 Athens Olympics theme). It is a slow singalong.
3. **伏線回収** (2024): has an official dance and was a "festival-like" choreography moment on the 2025 arena tour. Alternatives with official dances are **タッタ** and **イロトリドリ**. **ビューティフル** is a clap / call-and-response song.

**참여 유형**
- **夏色**: the crowd plays tambourines (or claps), and the whole venue sings along. There is a fixed call: the audience shouts "もう1回! もう1回!" and Kitagawa answers "バカ野郎!" before the slow-down repeat of the last chorus.
  - 2025 report: https://realsound.jp/2025/03/post-1951822_2.html (fetched)
  - Older call description: https://www.oricon.co.jp/special/51505/ (search snippet)
- **ビューティフル**: opens a cappella over the audience's handclaps, then Kitagawa shouts "カモン!" and the crowd sings back (same realsound report).
- **伏線回収**: the whole venue does the official dance (same report).
- **栄光の架橋**: mostly listening, with a singalong in the chorus.
- **Frontier**: flag waving.
- **Chururi**: the crowd sings the chorus.

**기존 자료**
- Official YUZU dance lectures (Japanese video; they show the moves but no timestamped timing):
  - 伏線回収 ダンス解説 レクチャー: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l_rfjay6OE0. Choreography is by 花村想太 (Da-iCE) × Shungo. There is also a TikTok by Kitagawa: https://www.tiktok.com/@yujin_kitagawa/video/7397630409149844744
  - タッタ ダンス講座: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qNHfwt9hIf0
  - マスカット スカッとダンス レクチャー (choreography by ラッキィ池田): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GE2ICwczuoE
  - イロトリドリ 振付レクチャー (2013), announced at https://x.com/yuzu_official/status/300606812183490560
  - Official channel (it says it has dance lectures): https://www.youtube.com/@yuzuofficial
- Live reports in Japanese, prose only with no timing: realsound 2025 (above), and Kアリーナ 2023 https://realsound.jp/2023/10/post-1449951.html
- Korean material: none.

**신뢰도: HIGH** for the dance songs (official lecture videos exist). MEDIUM for 夏色 (the call is well documented in prose, but I found no timing guide).

## 2. SUKIMASWITCH (スキマスイッチ)

Korea history:
- WONDERLIVET 2025 (2025-11-15).
- First solo Korea show, "POPMAN'S WORLD 2026 in Seoul", 2026-06-20 and 21 at YES24 Wonderloch Hall. Headlines were about the 떼창 (crowd singalong).

**대표곡 후보**
1. **全力少年**: the big singalong song. It closed the WONDERLIVET 2025 set before 奏 and was the Sunday encore in Seoul.
2. **奏 (かなで)**: their best-known ballad. It closed both the WONDERLIVET 2025 set and the Seoul main set. Listening, with a singalong.
3. **ボクノート** and **Ah Yeah!!** (Haikyuu!! OP), plus **ゴールデンタイムラバー** (FMA OP). The anime songs are the ones Korean fans know.

WONDERLIVET 2025 setlist (namu, fetched): LINE / ガラナ / 雫 / Revival / ボクノート / ゴールデンタイムラバー / Ah Yeah!! / 全力少年 / 奏.

**참여 유형**
- **全力少年**: call-and-response and singalong. In Japan, reports describe it as the "もうちょっと騒ぐか!" moment (https://www.thefirsttimes.jp/?p=541682, snippet only).
- **Ah Yeah!!** and **ゴールデンタイムラバー**: fast songs with clapping and fist raising. I found no documented fixed calls.
- **奏** and **ボクノート**: mostly listening, singalong in the chorus.

**기존 자료**
- Korean news on the Seoul show (prose, no timing):
  - https://m.etnews.com/20260622000018 (fetched; partial setlist; says Korean fans sang the Japanese lyrics)
  - https://www.koreadaily.com/article/20260622000604430
  - https://www.insight.co.kr/news/547921
- Korean fan blog posts: none found through search. Naver blogs are probably not indexed.
- Japanese material on how to join in: none specific.

**신뢰도: MEDIUM.** The setlists are solid and the Korean crowd sang along, but there is no documented clap or call pattern.

## 3. Ai Otsuka (大塚愛)

Korea history: I found no Korea show. In Korea, さくらんぼ is known through Kim Junsu's cover and a streamer-reaction meme (https://www.wikitree.co.kr/articles/599873).

**대표곡 후보**
1. **さくらんぼ**: by far the best known, and her usual closing song.
2. **プラネタリウム**: a ballad. Its THE FIRST TAKE video has 4.2M+ views.
3. **SMILY**, or 金魚花火. SMILY was re-recorded in 2025 as "SMILY2".

**참여 유형**
- **さくらんぼ**: clapping, plus the audience shouting "もう1回!" with the lyric. At the end she hands the "もう1回" to the crowd ("「もう1回」と言うのは、あなたの番です!").
  - https://www.billboard-japan.com/d_news/detail/34441/2 (snippet)
  - https://utaten.com/news/index/21129 (snippet)
- A dcinside post from Japan (she appeared as a guest at an Aimyon show) mentions a さくらんぼ 떼창: https://gall.dcinside.com/m/aimyon/31442
- **プラネタリウム**: listening.

**기존 자료**
- Official live video: さくらんぼ (LOVE IS BORN 15th 2018) https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GbbTS40TjNc. It shows the crowd reaction but gives no instructions.
- THE FIRST TAKE: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AQezyVAJL7Q (studio, not useful for participation).
- Japanese or Korean guide to how to join in: none.

**신뢰도: MEDIUM-LOW.** The "もう1回" call is a known convention, but nothing documents its timing.

## 5. nishina (にしな)

## 6. jo0ji

Korea history: none. This will be the first Korea appearance. A Korean search for "조지" returns Joji, a different artist, so watch the naming.

**대표곡 후보**
1. **よあけのうた**: ED of Jujutsu Kaisen S3 ("死滅回游 前編"), which gives it the most name recognition in Korea.
2. **不屈に花**: his breakout song and the opener of the 2025-26 tour.
3. **雨酔い**: led a full-venue singalong to close the main set. **条司** (arms raised and swaying) is an alternative.

**참여 유형**
- **雨酔い**: singalong.
- **Nukui**: the crowd sings the repeated English refrain.
- **条司**: arms raised and swayed side to side in the chorus.
- Most other songs are for listening.
- Source: the Zepp Shinjuku 2026-01-17 report with full setlist, https://www.thefirsttimes.jp/?p=754164 (fetched). Also https://www.billboard-japan.com/d_news/detail/157741/2

**기존 자료**
- Japanese live reports only. No Korean material, no videos with crowd guidance, no timing.

**신뢰도: LOW.** There is one good report, but his audience mostly listens.

## Overall observations
1. YUZU is the only act in this group with official, repeatable choreography material: dance lecture videos for 伏線回収, タッタ, マスカット and イロトリドリ, plus fixed rituals in 夏色 ("もう1回" / tambourine). Their page could be the showcase "振り付け" page.
2. For SUKIMASWITCH, natori, eill and Ai Otsuka, the documented participation is mostly singalong (떼창), with a few fixed calls (さくらんぼ "もう1回", Seoul フィナーレ。 in Korean). For these, the site's value is lyric singalong practice (romanized/Korean pronunciation of the chorus) rather than moves.
3. yama, Tani Yuuki, jo0ji and nishina have almost no documented participation conventions. Their audiences mostly listen. The site should label them honestly (감상 위주 / 후렴 따라부르기). For these artists, prior-Korea setlists (WONDERLIVET 2024 and 2025, setlist.fm) are better sourced than any participation data.
4. Korean fan write-ups (Naver blogs, theqoo) hardly surfaced through web search. Korean news articles exist for the SUKIMASWITCH and natori solo shows, but none gives timing. If timing matters, someone will have to build it from official live videos.
