# Extra call research: bands (WONDERLIVET 2026)

Research date: 2026-11-10. Sources were fetched to check them. Cues are paraphrased from the sources and nothing was invented.

**Coverage caveat:** the session-wide WebSearch budget (200 calls) ran out partway through. SAMBOMASTER was the last artist searched normally. CLAN QUEEN, Chevon, QWER and Conton Candy were checked only by fetching known pages: namu.wiki, realsound tag pages and live reports, and adorehack. None of them got a Japanese or Korean keyword search. Treat their X results as "not found in a limited check", not "confirmed nothing exists". A follow-up pass with search is recommended for these.

Classification:
- Q1 = cues tied to positions in the song
- Q2 = at least one specific fixed cue from a source
- X = only generic, or nothing found

### Red Criminal: Q2 (weak, one report)

## [Alexandros]

### Girl A: X
- Kawakami shouts "全員跳べますか！" (THIS FES '26).
- adorehack: people jump to the heavy four-on-the-floor beat (VIP PARTY '25).
- A Budokan show in the COVID era had clapping and raised arms.
- No fixed shout, clap point or singalong found.
- Sources:
  - https://www.thefirsttimes.jp/report/0000910682/
  - https://adorehack.com/article/alexandros-live-songs/

### ENDROLL (with SennaRin / Sawano Hiroyuki): Q2
- The song starts with a crowd singalong of the opening a cappella part. Kawakami points the mic at the audience to start it.
- Seen at the YOU ARE WELCOME TOUR final at Zepp Haneda (realsound, 2026-09).
- adorehack also says it begins with the floor singing ("客席のシンガロングから始まり"), with voices joining naturally when the mic is turned to the crowd.
- A position-tied cue (the opening section), but it is the only one.
- Sources:
  - https://realsound.jp/2026/09/post-2513494.html
  - https://adorehack.com/article/alexandros-live-songs/
  - https://www.thefirsttimes.jp/report/0000910682/

## SAMBOMASTER

### ヒューマニティ！: Q2
- Kyoto Daisakusen 2026 (BARKS):
  - Before the song, Yamaguchi asks "レゲエのリズムで踊ってくれる人？".
  - Drummer Kiuchi shouts "ハイ！ハイ！ハイ！" while playing, and the crowd claps along.
- adorehack: clapping starts from the intro (Victor Rock Matsuri), and Yamaguchi shouts "踊りまくれ！".
- Sources:
  - https://barks.jp/news/1075926/
  - https://adorehack.com/article/sambomaster-live-songs/

### Future is Yours: X
- Usually played just before 「できっこないを やらなくちゃ」, and Yamaguchi gets the crowd to jump high around it (BARKS).
- adorehack: raise hands and sing along in the chorus. Generic.
- Sources:
  - https://barks.jp/news/1075926/
  - https://adorehack.com/article/sambomaster-live-songs/

### 花束: Q2
- The crowd sings the chorus line containing "花束" (adorehack).
- At the Yokohama Arena one-man show, 11,581 people held flowers handed out at the venue and sang it together (SPICE). The flowers were a one-off for that show.
- Also a big handclap at K-Arena, raised fists while singing, and Yamaguchi shouting "誰が花束なのか、まだわからねえのか！？" (BARKS).
- The usual closing song.
- Sources:
  - https://adorehack.com/article/sambomaster-live-songs/
  - https://spice.eplus.jp/articles/324009
  - https://barks.jp/news/1075926/
