# Extra call research: J-pop set staples (WONDERLIVET 2026)

Research date: 2026-11-10. Read-only web research. Every cue below was checked against the fetched page text. No URLs or cues are invented.

**Caveat:** the session's WebSearch budget ran out partway through (shared limit of 200 calls). The jo0ji songs got only one search. **eill** and **Kyary Pamyu Pamyu** got none: for those I only fetched Wikipedia pages directly. Korean searches (내한 후기 / 떼창 / 응원법) were run for natori and Ai Otsuka only. eill (Seoul 2023/2024/2026, WONDERLIVET 2024) and Kyary are the main gaps to fill with more searching.

Classification: Q1 = cues at named positions in the song; Q2 = at least one specific fixed cue documented; X = generic statements or nothing.
"Q2 (weak)" = a specific action is documented, but its position in the song is not, or it is official choreography with no evidence that crowds do it.

## YUZU

### 栄光の架橋: X (borderline)
- Every report describes a whole-song mass singalong: "5万人での大合唱" (Oricon), "LAKESIDE STAGE全体でこの曲を合唱" (Real Sound 2017), and "会場は冒頭から大合唱" (crank-in, わっかフェス2025). crank-in also says the audience and YUZU sang in turns, but no source names the lines.
- No named section, clap or motion. In practice the song is a full singalong, often the closing number.
- Sources:
  - https://www.crank-in.net/trend/report/163851/2
  - https://realsound.jp/2017/08/post-103951.html
  - https://www.oricon.co.jp/special/49606/ (the page text was garbled when fetched, so the quote is from the search snippet only)

### サヨナラバス: Q2
- **Chorus (サビ): wave hands left and right.** crank-in (わっかフェス2025): 「サビ中に観客が左右に手を振り大盛り上がりとなった。」
- Real Sound (SWEET LOVE SHOWER 2017): 「リズムに合わせてオーディエンスが手を左右に振る」
- Real Sound (2019 弾き語り dome tour): 「「サヨナラバス」は冒頭からオーディエンスの大合唱」 (singalong from the first line).
- Sources:
  - https://www.crank-in.net/trend/report/163851/2
  - https://realsound.jp/2017/08/post-103951.html
  - https://realsound.jp/2019/07/post-385982.html

## natori

### Serenade (セレナーデ): X
- Opener at the 2026 Budokan shows. scrmbl: "That had the crowd bouncing up and down". That is a generic description, with no fixed cue.
- Sources:
  - https://scrmbl.com/post/natori-nippon-budokan-2026-live-report
  - https://realsound.jp/2026/03/post-2314613.html

### Propose (プロポーズ): Q2 (weak)
- **Handclaps.** The Sony Music official Budokan report says: 「切ないメロディに乗せてハンドクラップが鳴り響く。」 The report does not say which section.
- Source: https://www.sonymusic.co.jp/artist/natori/info/581322

### 非常口逃げてみた: X
- Played as a Yohji Igarashi remix at Budokan. THE FIRST TIMES calls the reaction 「カオティックな盛り上がり」, which is generic.
- Sources:
  - https://www.thefirsttimes.jp/?p=767933
  - https://realsound.jp/2026/03/post-2314613.html

### Overdose: Q2 (weak)
- **Intro: scream and raise both hands, swaying to the beat.** SETN (Taipei show): 「〈Overdose〉前奏一下，全場歌迷瞬間爆發尖叫聲，紛紛高舉雙手跟著節奏搖擺」
- At Budokan, natori repeatedly urged the floor on with 「全力でかかってこい！」 (Sony official report). That is the artist hyping the crowd, not a fixed audience cue.
- A TikTok dance by localcampione and others exists (entx article). No source shows a live crowd doing it.
- A search snippet said the first Seoul show (Nov 2025) had an explosive 떼창 on this song. That was not verified on the page; the supple.kr article I fetched names no songs.
- Sources:
  - https://www.mirrormedia.mg/external/setn_1886729
  - https://www.sonymusic.co.jp/artist/natori/info/581322
  - https://www.ticket.co.jp/entx/?p=1033

### 絶対零度: X
- Often the last song. Reports only say "the crowd roars along / hopping along" (scrmbl), 「オーディエンスを暴れさせた」 (Real Sound), and that it re-ignited the crowd in Taipei (SETN).
- In a Rolling Stone Korea interview (reposted on Postype), natori says he felt it had become "a song everyone can sing together" at the Seoul show. That is generic singalong, with no named section.
- Sources:
  - https://scrmbl.com/post/natori-nippon-budokan-2026-live-report
  - https://www.postype.com/@kimnatto/post/21839064
  - https://realsound.jp/2026/03/post-2314613.html
- Side note for the Seoul crowd: during member introductions, fans chanted "나토리 나토리!" (Postype interview). This is not tied to any song.

### グローリー: Q2 (weak)
- **The artist calls 「歌って」 and the whole crowd sings.** ぴあ (3rd album free live at 北谷公園, 2026-03-22): 「『グローリー』では『歌って』と呼びかけ、再び全体で歌唱をした。」 Afterwards she said: 「初めてみんなに歌ってもらえて…」
- The song was built for communal singing: the MV is stitched together from fan-submitted videos of people singing it. No source names a specific singalong section.
- Sources:
  - https://lp.p.pia.jp/article/news/461301/index.html
  - https://lp.p.pia.jp/article/news/459376/index.html

## jo0ji

### 不屈に花: X
- Tour opener. Before the last chorus, jo0ji sings a cappella alone in a spotlight. That is staging, not an audience cue. No audience participation is described.
- Sources:
  - https://www.thefirsttimes.jp/?p=754164
  - https://www.billboard-japan.com/d_news/detail/157741/2

### よあけのうた: X
- It was premiered in the encore at Zepp Shinjuku (Jan 2026). No audience cue.
- Same sources as 不屈に花.

### 眼差し: X
- The only mention is 「懐かしいメロディが輝く「眼差し」」. No audience cue.
- Same sources as 不屈に花.
- For context, jo0ji crowd cues documented in the same reports are on other songs: in 「条司」 the crowd raises arms and sways left/right on the chorus, and in 「雨酔い」 jo0ji leads a full-crowd singalong.

## Kyary Pamyu Pamyu (Wikipedia only, because the search budget ran out)

### 君に100パーセント: X
- The official title is 「キミに100パーセント」 (2012 single 「キミに100パーセント/ふりそでーしょん」). Wikipedia says no official MV was made: 「公式ミュージックビデオ（MV）は制作されていない」. No live cue was found.
- Source: https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/キミに100パーセント/ふりそでーしょん

### にんじゃりばんばん: Q2 (weak, choreography only)
- Official choreography exists: choreographer MAIKO, whose dancing was motion-captured for the robot CG in the MV. A cited headline mentions a 「100万DLの振付ビデオ初収録」 (choreography video included with the release).
- Wikipedia does not describe the moves, and no source shows a live crowd doing them. Do not transcribe moves without watching the official video.
- Source: https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/にんじゃりばんばん

### 原宿いやほい: Q2 (weak, choreography only)
- Official choreography uses a Cossack-dance motif: 「振り付けは コサックダンス をモチーフにしたもの」.
- No source documents an audience shout of "いやほい" or a crowd doing the dance.
- Source: https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/原宿いやほい
