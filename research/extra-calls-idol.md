## Da-iCE

General context:
- Fans chant 「Da-iCE Da-iCE」 for the encore, and a towel-swinging song exists. Source: a fan answer on Yahoo!知恵袋, https://detail.chiebukuro.yahoo.co.jp/qa/question_detail/q12321955982. The other cues on that page come from AI-generated answers and are unreliable.
- The crowd danced along to a hand-gesture dance in 「オレンジユース」, which is not on our list. Sources: https://www.thefirsttimes.jp/report/0000532805/ and https://avexnet.jp/news/1020114

### スターマイン: X (borderline)
What sources do document, none of it a fixed cue at a lyric position:
- At テレ朝『The Performance』, 「スターマイン」 was the last song. Hanamura's 「立ち上がれ!!」 got everyone standing, and 「観客全体が大合唱するシーンもあった」. Afterward came an 「おかわり」 bit: they sang a short phrase again after saying goodbye, repeated at least 3 times, with FANTASTICS joining on the 3rd. The article does not say which section was repeated or who asked for it. Source: https://www.thefirsttimes.jp/report/0000410836/
- A blog says the crowd jumps, including people who don't know Da-iCE: 「飛び跳ね熱気に包まれます」. It also says to watch the 「くだり」 after the song ends, without describing it. This is probably the おかわり bit, which is my inference. Source: https://toyotoyu.com/daice-live-popular-songs-top10/
- Choreography: Kudo said the chorus was made exactly 15 seconds long for TikTok, and the choreography was by shoji of s**t kingz. The interview does not say whether the audience dances along live. Source: https://rockinon.com/interview/detail/204616. One search summary instead credited the choreography to ローカルカンピオーネ (search-summary only, not verified).
- Placement: a staple song around the encore (https://motoryokoblog.com/?p=21607). It opened the encore of the 10th-anniversary tour (thefirsttimes 532805, avexnet).
- Recommendation: there is no verified cue to put in the guide. At most: "jump / sing along in the chorus; stay for the おかわり after the song."

### CITRUS: X
- Only setlist mentions, and the note that 「会場全体が聴き入っていた」 at The Performance, i.e. a listening song. Sources: thefirsttimes 410836, motoryokoblog 11189/21607.

## ReoNa

General context:
- Fans chant 「ReoNa」 while waiting before the set, and calls for her continue after the set. Sources: https://www.thefirsttimes.jp/report/0000856801/ and https://koubo.jp/article/83728
- The crowd-participation moments reported are for other songs:
  - forget-me-not: a big sing-along in Thailand, or quiet listening while swaying penlights in Kuala Lumpur
  - ANIMA: the biggest sing-along of the show, continuing into the outro
  - 生命線: red penlights
  - Sources: the same two reports
- The namu.wiki ReoNa page (https://namu.wiki/w/ReoNa) has no concert-culture section.

### ないない: X
- It is often the set opener. At the Thailand show the crowd cheered at the harpsichord intro (koubo.jp/article/83728), a one-off reaction rather than a cue. It debuted with a chorus and strings on the unknown tour (https://realsound.jp/2021/05/post-757946.html), and no participation is described there.
