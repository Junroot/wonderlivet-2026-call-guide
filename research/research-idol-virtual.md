# WONDERLIVET 2026: Idol / Virtual / Anime group research (11 acts)

Research date: 2026-11-10. Read-only web research. Everything below comes from fetched pages or search-result snippets. Anything marked **(unverified)** comes from a single secondary source or a snippet I could not open.

## Lineup / day confirmation

- Day and stage per namu.wiki [WONDERLIVET 2026](https://namu.wiki/w/WONDERLIVET%202026). The stage was inferred from column order, so check it against the official timetable:
  - **Day 1, 11/20 (Fri):** Hoshimachi Suisei (WONDER), Da-iCE (WONDER), ReoNa (LIVET)
  - **Day 2, 11/21 (Sat):** MyGO!!!!! (WONDER), Roselia (WONDER), Kyary Pamyu Pamyu (WONDER), TAK with Hatsune Miku & Kasane Teto (LIVET; TAK's own X post confirms "LIVET STAGE"), CHiCO with HoneyWorks (LIVET)
  - **Day 3, 11/22 (Sun):** KUROMI (WONDER), CANDY TUNE (WONDER), SWEET STEADY (LIVET)
- The early press articles (khan.co.kr 2026-08-29, iMBC timetable) do not name ReoNa, Da-iCE, Kyary or CHiCO; only namu.wiki lists them. One namu.wiki note (on the 일본 가수 마이너 갤러리 page) also mentions a lineup version "without ReoNa" dated 8/28. Confirm these four against the official LIVET_KR announcements before building pages for them.
- Festival format: all standing across two stages. Each act gets a festival-length set (typically 6-8 songs), so the "safe" songs are the ones each act plays at other festivals.

## 1. Hoshimachi Suisei (hololive), Day 1

**대표곡 후보**
- **ビビデバ**: closer of every 2026 Once Upon a Stellar arena show, opener of SuperNova: REBOOT, and last song of her ROCK IN JAPAN 2026 set (9/19). Phone filming has been allowed during this song on the 2026 tour.
- **Stellar Stellar**: her signature song. In the 2026 arena tour setlist and the RIJ 2026 set.
- Third pick: **ソワレ**, **もうどうなってもいいや** or **NEXT COLOR PLANET**. ソワレ and もうどうなってもいいや were both in the RIJ 2026 6-song festival set (Stellar Stellar / もうどうなってもいいや / プリマドンナ / GUM&DROP / ソワレ / ビビデバ). That set is the best model for a festival setlist.

**참여 유형**
- Penlights: **blue** (her image color). Arena reports describe the hall "turning blue". Official penlights have 8 colors and change per song in her own productions.
- C&R: 「すいちゃんは〜?」「今日もかわいい〜!」. There is an **official rule**: do this call only when Suisei herself prompts it, and not before the show or 10 minutes before start (her X post [x.com/suisei_hosimati/status/1867547529363292470](https://x.com/suisei_hosimati/status/1867547529363292470)). A later post reportedly tightened this to "only when prompted", including before and after the show **(unverified via metaversesokuhou summary)**. The guide should include this rule.
- Clap: clap before the chorus in Stellar Stellar. ビビデバ has a 「ビビデバ」 call, and clapping in its interlude is led from the stage (animatetimes official report).
- Songs with calls per a Chiebukuro answer: the 先駆者 intro, part of ソワレ, the bracketed parts of NEXT COLOR PLANET, the repeats in 繭と心.

**기존 자료**
- No comprehensive per-song call sheet (コール表) was found in Japanese or Korean.
- [Yahoo!知恵袋 q13325447788](https://detail.chiebukuro.yahoo.co.jp/qa/question_detail/q13325447788): JP, Q&A listing which songs have calls. No timing. Low.
- [motoryokoblog 予習ガイド](https://motoryokoblog.com/?p=11884): JP, list of standard setlist songs. No calls.
- [lignea.co.jp/suisei-setlist](https://lignea.co.jp/suisei-setlist/): JP, 2026 tour setlist (20 songs). [ja.wikipedia Once Upon a Stellar](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/HOSHIMACHI_SUISEI_ARENA_TOUR_2026_Once_Upon_a_Stellar) has the full setlist.
- [utaten RIJ 2026 9/19 setlist](https://utaten.com/live/rock-in-japan-festival-2026-setlist-0919/): festival setlist.
- [seesaawiki hololive 非公式wiki ペンライト page](https://seesaawiki.jp/hololivetv/d/%A5%DA%A5%F3%A5%E9%A5%A4%A5%C8): JP, penlight info (not opened).
- Korean: [dcinside 호시마치 스이세이 마이너 갤러리](https://m.dcinside.com/board/hosimati/49429) has Wonderlivet posts but no call guide found. [arca.live 홀로라이브 채널: 원더리벳2025 tips](https://arca.live/b/holopro/181303352) covers venue tips only. The namu.wiki "방송 일화" page explains the background of the 今日もかわいい call ban (Korean).

**신뢰도: medium.** Penlight color and C&R rule come from an official source. Per-song calls are fragmentary.

## 2. MyGO!!!!! (BanG Dream!), Day 2. First Korea show.

**대표곡 후보**
- **迷星叫**: debut song, played at every major live (e.g. 6th LIVE DAY2).
- **影色舞**: the crowd's most active song. Fans copy the members' dance steps and clap at set points (dengekionline 6th LIVE report; Cocoreo note).
- **碧天伴走** or **焚音打**: 碧天伴走 is a staple and the anime insert song. In 焚音打, fans raise fists before the last chorus on 羊宮妃那's cue.
- I could not get the 9th LIVE (July 2026) setlist. It is in a bushiroad PDF ([bushiroad.com/media/f371acb8d3b3d434](https://bushiroad.com/media/f371acb8d3b3d434)) that I could not parse.

**참여 유형**
- Mostly clap, 振りコピ (影色舞), fist raises (音一会, 焚音打) and crowd responses to MC prompts. MyGO has few or no classic idol-style コール.
- Penlights: the band color is sky blue (#3388BB per moegirl), and most of the crowd uses it.
- Member colors per a fan X post ([x.com/BanGDreamGH/status/1644977937517805568](https://x.com/BanGDreamGH/status/1644977937517805568)): 燈 水色, 愛音 pink, 楽奈 green, そよ yellow, 立希 purple. Hex values per gearfaves (unofficial): 燈 #77BBDD, 愛音 #FF8899. One note blog says 燈 blue and 愛音 red, which conflicts. Verify against the official bang-dream.com character pages.

**기존 자료**
- **No MyGO コール表 found** in Japanese or Korean. The big BanG Dream call site [バンドリ！ポッピンコール！](https://bangdream.seesaa.net/) does not cover MyGO.
- [dengekionline 6th LIVE DAY2 report](https://dengekionline.com/article/202408/12527): JP, setlist plus crowd participation (claps in 影色舞, fist raise in 焚音打).
- [note: Cocoreo "MyGO!!!!!のライブ"](https://note.com/cocoreo308/n/na28a2296bef9): JP, crowd culture essay.
- Korean: [namu.wiki MyGO!!!!!](https://namu.wiki/w/MyGO!!!!!) records the first Korea show and fan ads. Ruliweb: [Wonderlivet billboard post](https://bbs.ruliweb.com/family/4526/board/300143/read/77057208) and [comment video](https://bbs.ruliweb.com/news/board/1002/read/2438550). The dcinside [BanG Dream 마이너 갤러리](https://gall.dcinside.com/mgallery/board/lists/?id=bang_dream) is the most likely place for a Wonderlivet call guide to appear, but none was found as of today.

**신뢰도: low.** Material is limited to live reports and fan color lists. You would have to create the participation guide yourself from official live videos.

## 3. Roselia (BanG Dream!), Day 2

**대표곡 후보**
- **FIRE BIRD**: last encore at Lehre der Rose (Ariake, 2026-08-29/30) and at Seoul (2025-12-24).
- **BLACK SHOUT**: debut single. Encore at Lehre DAY2 and in the Seoul setlist.
- **Neo-Aspect**: opened both Lehre days, and the Korean 떼창 (chorus singalong) on it was the most praised moment at the Seoul show (namu.wiki). Alternates: **PASSIONATE ANTHEM** (both Lehre days, Seoul EN2) or **R**.
- Seoul 2025 setlist: FRONTIER FANTASIA / Song I am. / Determination Symphony / 紫炎 / R / 熱色スターマイン / Ringing Bloom / 陽だまりロードナイト / 軌跡 / BLACK SHOUT / Steadfast Spirits / Neo-Aspect / EN FIRE BIRD / PASSIONATE ANTHEM.

The names were garbled in the fetch, so the member mapping below is the standard order and should be verified: 友希那 violet, 紗夜 turquoise, リサ red, あこ magenta/pink, 燐子 silver. dcinside confirms あこ = pink.

## 4. TAK with Hatsune Miku & Kasane Teto (3D virtual stage), Day 2

TAK (Han Wontak, aka DORIDORI) is a Korean K-pop producer and DJ who debuted as a Vocaloid producer in Aug 2024.

**대표곡 후보**
- **PPPP** (feat. Miku & Teto, 2025-09): his biggest hit, over 60M YouTube views per vocaloid fandom. It contains a Korean rap phrase that Korean fans already quote.
- **LEMON MELON COOKIE** (feat. Miku): over 12M views. Added to Project SEKAI in Oct 2026, covered by the unit MORE MORE JUMP!.
- **mochimochi** (debut) or **Numb Numb** (Miku and Teto, WIT STUDIO MV, May 2026).
- **No setlist found.** I found no evidence that TAK has performed live with Miku and Teto before, so this may be the first such show.

**참여 유형**
- No established calls for TAK songs.
- General Miku-concert conventions apply. Per the official Magical Mirai beginner guide ([magicalmirai.com/2025/info_beginners-guide.html](https://magicalmirai.com/2025/info_beginners-guide.html)), Miku's color is blue-green (teal), and fans copy the waving pattern of the people around them. Teto's color is commonly red (her character color). No penlight source for Teto was found, so this is unverified.

**기존 자료**
- No call materials for TAK songs. Context only: [Real Sound TAK interview (2025-12)](https://realsound.jp/2025/12/post-2258252.html), [初音ミクWiki TAK](https://w.atwiki.jp/hmiku/pages/65900.html), [MV PPPP 立体音響ver.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Uv36-VxcV_k), and TAK's X account [@TAK_DRDR](https://x.com/tak_drdr).
- Adjacent Miku call culture (for a "how Miku shows work" section): [note: マジカルミライ2025 CD曲コール一覧](https://note.com/tunadayo/n/n1dee539c129b) (JP).

**신뢰도: none** for song-specific material. Low for general Miku etiquette.

## 5. CANDY TUNE (KAWAII LAB.), Day 3

**대표곡 후보**
- **倍倍FIGHT!**: the TikTok hit, performed on Kohaku. Closed the Budokan 2026 main set, and they played it as surprise guests at Steve Aoki's MIDNIGHT SONIC 2026.
- **キス・ミー・パティシエ**: 1st CD single, played at Budokan 2026.
- **必殺あざとポーズ** or **レベチかわいい！**: both are popular. HAPPY BOUNCE BIRTHDAY is the Budokan singalong.

**참여 유형**

Also an official choreography video: [Choreography Video -MV edition-](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xidmqFtyL0w). MV: [9HKbo1FstOE](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9HKbo1FstOE).

## 6. SWEET STEADY (KAWAII LAB.), Day 3

**대표곡 후보**
- **なんてねっ！**: the fans' favorite live song. Has an official call video. Note the difficult double-speed triplet MIX after verse 2.
- **ぱじゃまぱーてぃー！**: their best-known song, and it needs very few calls (mostly 「教えて！」 plus raised hands in the chorus). Good for beginners.
- **SWEET STEP** (2026-02, latest single, official call video) or **始まりの合図** (debut song).
- Recent shows: Pia Arena MM (2026-08-23, sold out); Makuhari (2026-04, all 23 songs). Full setlists were not obtained.

**참여 유형**
- Idol コール/MIX, member-name calls, スイステ口上.
- Member colors (ciatr): 奥田彩友 blue, 音井結衣 light blue, 栗田なつか pink, 塩川莉世 purple, 庄司なぎさ orange, 白石まゆみ red, 山内咲奈 yellow. Mint green is retired since 柳川みあ graduated in July 2024.
